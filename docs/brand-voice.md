# Brand Voice — EcoGreen Solar

## Tone
Warm, community-focused, approachable, transparent. Melbourne-local, not corporate.

## Language Rules
- "Melbourne's Trusted Solar & Battery Experts" — always the tagline
- Use "we" / "our" / "your" — never "EcoGreen" in third person
- Reference Melbourne suburbs, Victoria contexts, local incentives
- "Free Consultation" / "Get a Quote" as primary CTAs
- Avoid jargon — "solar panels" not "PV modules", "battery" not "ESS"

## Do ✅
- "We've helped 500+ Melbourne homes go solar"
- "From Frankston to Craigieburn, we know Melbourne roofs"
- "Save on power bills with a custom system"

## Don't ❌
- "EcoGreen is a leading provider" (too corporate)
- "Our cutting-edge photovoltaic technology" (too jargony)
- Emojis in any content
